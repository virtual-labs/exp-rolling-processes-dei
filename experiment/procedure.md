### Procedure

1. Start the rolling process simulation or ensure that the rolling mill setup is ready for operation.

2. Select the material to be used for rolling (such as aluminium, copper, or steel) from the available options in the simulation.

3. Set the initial dimensions of the workpiece, including thickness and width, as per the given input conditions.

4. Choose the roll parameters such as roll diameter, rotational speed, and reduction per pass.

5. Set the velocity of the ram or feed mechanism according to the simulation requirements.

6. Initiate the rolling operation and allow the workpiece to enter the roll gap.

7. Observe the deformation of the metal as it passes between the rotating rolls and note the reduction in thickness and increase in length.

8. Monitor the rolling forces, roll pressure, and power consumption during the process.

9. Continue the rolling operation through the required number of passes to achieve the desired final thickness.

10. Stop the simulation and record the final dimensions, surface quality, and any defects observed in the rolled product.
